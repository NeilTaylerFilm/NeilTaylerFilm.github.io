Adding Photographs.

Upload images to Cloudflare R2 and get addresses you can use anywhere on your site.

What is R2? Cloudflare R2 is a storage service that holds your images and serves them fast. Think of it as a permanent photo album linked from your website.

Upload a single image

Step 1

Open the upload page

Navigate to: r2.dash.cloudflare.com

Step 2

Select your bucket

Click on the bucket named neiltaylerfilm.github.io in the left sidebar. If prompted for credentials, contact the website owner.

Step 3

Upload your file

  1. Click the Upload button at the top
  2. Choose one or more files from your Mac
  3. Wait for the upload progress bar to finish
Step 4

Get the image address

  1. Click the uploaded file to open its details
  2. Right-click the Address field and select Copy Address
  3. Paste the address wherever you need it (blog post, project, slideshow)

Upload large images in small batches (a dozen or so at a time). Uploading hundreds at once can cause failures.

Bulk upload multiple images

Option A — Drag and drop

In the bucket view, drag entire folders onto the page. Files are organized by folder name when they arrive.

Option B — Command line
rclone copy /path/to/folder \
  remote:bucket-name/

See the terminal guide below for prerequisites.

Using the command line (optional)

Best for uploading many photos at once. Requires a working rclone setup.

rclone copy /path/to/photos remote:neiltaylerfilm.github.io/

For setup instructions, see the full Cloudflare setup guide.

After uploading

Copy the image address and paste it into your post or project. The image will load automatically when the page is published.
