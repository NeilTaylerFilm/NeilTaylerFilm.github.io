I photographed musician [Asha Menon](https://www.instagram.com/ashamenonmusic/) during the recording session for "Only The Saved Can Save", an original song for our final-year university short film, "A Song To Be Murdered By". Asha had written most of the song before the session; we discussed how it would sit in the film as she continued working on it, with ideas from our director, Daniel Sawyer.
