A series of landscape photographs taken during mountain hiking trips in 2022. These images capture the raw beauty and majesty of mountain environments.

## About this project

Mountain landscapes offer a unique challenge for photographers. The changing light, unpredictable weather, and need for physical endurance require careful planning and patience.
