Some ideas need somewhere to sit before they know what they are. A notebook, a folder, a page on the internet. The useful part is having a place to return to them, without requiring every observation to become a finished argument.

## An open-ended collection

This sample entry introduces another category. Categories come directly from the writing, so the journal can grow into new subjects without changing its structure.

A note might become an article. A photograph might become the beginning of a project. Or they might remain exactly what they were: a record of something that seemed worth noticing.

That is enough of a reason to keep them.
