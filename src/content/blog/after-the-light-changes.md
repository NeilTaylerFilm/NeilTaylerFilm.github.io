The familiar can become unfamiliar surprisingly quickly. Shop windows turn into small stages. Pavements pick up reflections. The space between buildings becomes a shape of its own. At night, a street can suggest an entirely different photograph from the one you noticed that afternoon.

## Follow what is already there

This is an example entry using a sample photograph, not a description of an assignment. It shows a second image-led post without a subtitle.

Look for a balance between the lit and unlit parts of a scene. Preserving darkness can be as important as retaining detail in a bright window. Let the image contain a few questions.

## Let the sequence breathe

Not every frame needs the same intensity. A quiet detail can give a more dramatic image somewhere to land. Try ordering a small set without worrying too much about chronology.
